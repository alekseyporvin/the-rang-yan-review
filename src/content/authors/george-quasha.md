---
name: "Quasha, George"
shortBio: "American poet, artist, and musician, born in White Plains, New York. With artist Susan Quasha he founded Station Hill Press in Barrytown, New York. His books include *Somapoetics*, *Ainu Dreams* and, of the nine books in his unique genre “preverbs” over twenty-five years, *Not Even Rabbits Go Down This Hole*, *Hearing Other*, and the forthcoming *Hilaritas Sublime*. He has held fellowships from the National Endowment for the Arts and the Guggenheim Foundation."
