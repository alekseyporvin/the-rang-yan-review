<p><strong>Your Pain</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--armantrout">Crater under the breast-bone,

seat of emotion
and digestion,

a mouth that can’t be fed.

<span class="armantrout-star">*</span>

You said you wanted
“Perma-Sleep” and laughed

as if death
were more land
for the market to claim.

<span class="armantrout-star">*</span>

Empathy is not
as large as pain.

It’s pain’s mascot.</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>Wink Wink</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--armantrout">A yellow Dead-End sign
behind high weeds

which she takes
for a sun flower.

<span class="armantrout-star">*</span>

She finds herself
quoting her own thoughts,

as signs.

<span class="armantrout-star">*</span>

Six tall flames
in a gas fire place
wince in sync

<span class="armantrout-star">*</span>

A game of freeze tag
between subatomic
<span class="armantrout-far"><span class="armantrout-pad" aria-hidden="true">between subatomic </span>pixels</span>

and observers
who don’t know they’re playing.</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>Time Management</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--armantrout"><span class="armantrout-num">1</span>

“I’m looking forward to life
being a little doll again.”

No doubt you mean ‘dull.’

Past and future are bumpers,
buffers.

Boredom is the distance
between now and then.

They say boredom
is only terror
when terror is just
beginning.

<span class="armantrout-num">2</span>

In my dreams there’s a trick to it.

<span class="armantrout-keep">At some point I realize that I have done what I am now doing on a previous occasion—</span>
<span class="armantrout-keep">in this case walking across an estuary on a mat of leaves and twigs, unsure that it will hold my weight. I remember the</span>
<span class="armantrout-keep">slimy sensation on my bare feet from my last crossing and feel encouraged</span>

as if time
<span class="armantrout-shift"><span class="armantrout-pad" aria-hidden="true">as if time </span>were a gentle</span>
push forward.</pre>
