fullBio: "She is the sole editor of *The Dreaming Machine* - Writings and visual arts from the world and maintains her personal blog Pina Piccolo’s blog /il blog di Pina Piccolo. In Italy she was a close collaborator for the journal *Sagarana*, and later one of the founders and editors of the literary journal *La Macchina Sognante*. Her Italian poetry collection *I canti dell’Interregno* was published by Lebeg edizioni in 2019; her English language poetry collection *Avatar songs* was published in 2026 by Lord Byron Ediciones; and her collection “Notes from the angel of chaos” is forthcoming in 2027 with Red River Press. The topics she has been exploring in recent years in her work revolve around language, ecology, philosophy and migration."
