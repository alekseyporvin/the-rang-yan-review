editorial: "Memory is not storage. It is a practice — selective, shaped by the present, subject to revision. The texts gathered in this issue approach memory as a political, ethical, and literary problem: What survives, and why? What is made to disappear, and by whom? What does it mean to carry the memory of others — of those who are gone, of those who cannot speak for themselves? We have gathered here poems of address and absence, fictions of small archives and large losses, essays on the politics of remembering and the necessity of forgetting. We offer them not as answers but as forms of sustained attention to a question that will not be resolved."
