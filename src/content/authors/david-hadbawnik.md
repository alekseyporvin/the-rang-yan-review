---
name: "Hadbawnik, David"
shortBio: "David Hadbawnik is a poet, translator, and scholar whose work brings together the study of premodern language and literature and a sustained engagement with twentieth-century avant-garde poetics. He holds a Ph.D. in medieval literature from SUNY Buffalo and has published several books of poetry, including *Field Work: Notes, Songs, and Poems, 1997–2010* (BlazeVOX), *Translations from Creeley* (Sardines Press), and *Ovid in Exile* (Interbirth Books). His scholarly writing includes essays on Edmund Spenser and Geoffrey Chaucer. He currently teaches at the American University of Kuwait."
