fullBio: "Miriam Hollander holds a doctorate in comparative literature from the University of Amsterdam and has spent two decades working at the intersection of literary scholarship, political thought, and editorial practice. Before founding *The Rangzen Review*, she edited several smaller publications and organized an international series of seminars on literature and emancipatory politics. Her scholarly work examines the role of literary magazines in the formation of political culture, with particular attention to the independent press of the twentieth century. She believes that journals like this one occupy a specific and irreplaceable position in cultural life: not the academy, not the market, but something between — a space for thinking that is too serious for entertainment and too committed for neutrality."
