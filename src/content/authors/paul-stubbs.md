shortBio: "Paul Stubbs is a British poet, editor, and mentor who lives between Chartres & England. He is the author of several poetry collections and books of poetical and philosophical essays. His work has appeared in various magazines and anthologies. With Blandine Longre, Stubbs has translated texts by Victor Segalen, Arthur Rimbaud, Jos Roy, and Pierre Cendors. He also co-founded the Anglo-French publishing house Black Herald Press in 2010. His latest book, released in November 2026, is *Preparations for the Afterlife: Selected Poems 1992-2025* (Broken Sleep Books). Forthcoming, *The Disappearance of the Poet: A Study on the Writings of Alice Oswald*."
