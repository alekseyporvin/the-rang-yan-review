<p><strong>to Cid Corman</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">like rain
washing these stones today
vanished tomorrow

these thoughts</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>baroque - on line</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">to Tom Raworth


1.
at deadline the picture
offers a wealth of openings
connections declaring
trends - a new civilization
now this process assumes
breaking characters - experiences
emerge - a statement of languages
propulsive strength of the heart
at once organic and coherent set
upon heaps of pedestals
of bases and plinths without reason

2.
aware of language with
negative tone - convinced
classicists - appear as
denial of order - unthinkable
abuses with teleprogrammed defense
swinging becomes ambiguous
elusive used with caution meant
as definition of a
manifold reality mostly
mauling - defaming - persecuting
crushed in the horror of war

3.
there is no wonder in
starting from these episodes a synthesis
rigid in the dead of night with
scenography of policemen - togas
reporters and defendants - fluttering
lolitas while the crime
is broadcast - an excess of ridicule
the pale mask fades out
while the speaker invites
an unsatisfied public
unconscious of complicities</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>for Gary Snyder</strong></p>

<div class="poem-epigraph">
<p>The blue mountains<br />
are constantly walking</p>
</div>

<pre class="poem-lines">farther away
the wild
side

dead fishes
on the lagoon
surface

a world
of water
into the water

you know
your land
your past

in the purified
blue vision
fragile self

to be nature
of nature
in nature

nail - plug
lamp
flower - branch - creek

everything is sacred
in the sacred
emptiness</pre>
