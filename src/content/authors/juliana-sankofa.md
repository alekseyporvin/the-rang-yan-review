---
name: "Sankofa, Juliana"
shortBio: "Brazilian poet, researcher, and professor from the state of Minas Gerais. Her writing explores ancestry, Black identity, memory, and resistance. Her poems were published in translation in *Afro-Hispanic Review* in 2024. She holds a PhD in Literary Studies, with research focused on the work of Black writer Miriam Alves and the relationships among literature, knowledge, and Afro-diasporic thought."
