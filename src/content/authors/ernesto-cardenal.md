shortBio: "Ernesto Cardenal (1925–2020) was a Nicaraguan poet, revolutionary, Catholic priest, and politician. Persecuted for his participation in the 1954 attempted overthrow of the Somoza dictatorship, he entered the Trappist Monastery of Gethsemani in Kentucky in 1957, where he was overseen by Thomas Merton, then master of novices. At the monastery, the monks’ choir sang the Vulgate version of the Psalms seven times per day. It was during that time that Cardenal was inspired to draft his own “translations,” which Merton later told him were “the versions we should really be singing in the choir.”"
