shortBio: "Mohsen Elblasy is a poet, translator, visual artist, and researcher whose work centers on surrealist literature and the visual arts in Egypt and the Middle East. He is the founder of Sulfur Editions, editor-in-chief of the surrealist magazine *The Room*, and co-founder of the Middle East and North Africa Surrealist Group."
fullBio: "He translates literary and philosophical texts, with particular attention to surrealist thought, and writes for international journals on art movements and contemporary work. His books include *Erotic, Political, Psalms* (Al Kotby, Egypt, 2014), *The Goddess of the Moon*, surrealist texts and poems written with Ghadah Kamal (Dar Badael, 2018), *The Free Imagination*, essays in literary and artistic criticism (Dar Badael, 2019), and *Rejection of the Poem*, an anthology and translation of historical surrealist and Dadaist poems (Rê, 2019). *The Trip of Kamel El-Telmissany: The Surrealist against the Realist* (New Culture, 2021) received the Saweras Prize for Criticism. With Ghadah Kamal he published *The Wolves of the Moon*, poetry and collages (La Belle Inutile, France, 2021). His translations of Joyce Mansour include *The Tuberose Baby Girl* (Sefsafa, 2024) and *Hurtful Stories: The Complete Prose Works of Joyce Mansour* (Al Mutawassit, Italy, 2024). He edited the surrealist anthology *IEOS* (La Belle Inutile, 2023).\n\nElblasy is executive director and general secretary of the International Cooperative for Arts, Sciences and Technology (I COAST) and co-founder of the Chrysopoeia Surrealist Union. He has directed the International Exhibition of Surrealism (Cairo / Saint-Cirq-Lapopie), curated *Echoes of Contemporary Surrealism* and *Maze of Games and Dreams* in Alexandria, and served as international coordinator of *Échos Surréalistes Contemporains* (Luxor, Saint-Cirq-Lapopie, Budapest, and Alabama), held in collaboration with the French Ministry of Culture."
