shortBio: "Aleksey Porvin is a poet, essayist, and literary critic. His areas of interest include the relationships between literature, politics, history, gender, and social experience, with particular attention to questions of class, inequality, ideology, and the politics of representation. His literary and critical interests also encompass the interplay of language, power, literary tradition, and collective subjectivity."
fullBio: "For his contribution to Russian letters, Porvin was awarded the Debut Prize (2012) and the Andrei Bely Prize (2025).\n\nThe Andrei Bely Prize is Russia's oldest independent literary award and the country's foremost recognition of the independent literary tradition that has developed outside both the Soviet literary establishment and state-sponsored ideological culture. Founded in 1978, it has, over the decades, honored many of the leading poets of Russian literature, establishing a distinguished record of recognizing writers of the first rank.\n\nAs Editor-in-Chief, he brings to the journal a commitment to politically engaged, formally innovative, and intellectually rigorous writing, with particular attention to voices and perspectives that challenge established cultural conventions."
