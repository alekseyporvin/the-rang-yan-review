<p><strong>Face</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">her face like smoke, like
all day having to take
a shit and something keeping you
from it

late afternoon smeared
with one swift trill of birdsong
and then farther off, a wave
lapping gently against

some other part of afternoon.
and the seesaw thinking
that shifts flowers back
and forth, a simple arrangement,

one letter mistaken for another,
the face he should not see
when he closes his eyes
but does</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>Truth</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">Read this quickly
and tell me the truth

think of the first word
and its antonym

think of the antonym
of the antonym

the first person makes an enemy
of the third

they go back to the second
stand there accusing each other

but it doesn’t end there
years later they barely recognize one another

are you happy?
are you telling the truth?</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>Men</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">men who talk to girls
in crowded hallways just before
the bell rings, because it’s

safe at that time, but
also a little risky. men
who sit down with

an open book in
a library, not doing
anything really, their eyes

restless though, and watching.
men who could die watching.
like a knot in a tree,

which measures time differently
and thus watches differently,
seeing whatever we see but

something beyond or within that,
a clotted sort of looking
with too many people inside</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>Qualms</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">I don’t doubt that he’s right,
but the thing of it is…
the thing of it is

that he plods too heavily
so heavily that it hangs there
each step of the way

in all the little blanks
and pauses, the thing
that he is and that’s

what’s giving me qualms,
not his hair, not the
bookshelf, not anything

you could really put
your finger on, but it
wouldn’t surprise me</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>Dark</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">each wave with a counterfeit shimmer
a bit of moonlight passed off
as the real thing

dagger covered in fake blood
drawn out of real flesh

letters that curl up and die
before reaching the lips

and through the tall grass
and the thickening dark
“do not awaken” written in haste</pre>
