---
name: "Venuti, Lawrence"
shortBio: "American translator, translation historian, and theorist (b. 1953, Philadelphia). Professor Emeritus of English at Temple University, he is the author of *The Translator’s Invisibility* (1995) and *The Scandals of Translation* (1998) and the editor of *The Translation Studies Reader*. He translates from Italian, French, and Catalan; his translations of Milo De Angelis include *Finite Intuition: Selected Poetry and Prose* (Sun & Moon Press, 1995). Recent work includes Dino Buzzati’s *The Bewitched Bourgeois* (NYRB Classics, 2025)."
