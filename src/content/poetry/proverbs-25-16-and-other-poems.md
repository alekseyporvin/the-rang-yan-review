<p class="baker-title baker-title--first">Proverbs 25:16</p>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--baker">I stop to wonder if
plenty has made you

ungrateful, as I have

too often lately found
myself; if removing honey

from its stingers, to eat
without knowing its only

origin, has torn enough
holes in the meadow for

it to forget about you.</pre>

<p class="baker-title">Proverbs 23:10</p>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--baker">Watch what they do.

Occupying forces will not
reveal all they remove
by force, but—video: an
IDF tank defacing ancient
al-Katiba park, landmark
of common green space—or,
video: the rapid encroach
of settlers, driven on
by desire to possess, the
bombed, burning fields—

We must speak truly of
how they became orphans.</pre>

<p class="baker-title">Dopamonic Possession</p>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--baker">so many books
so little time
infinite jest's
cognitive tithe
beckoning focus
withering stare
reckoning burgeons
glaucomic star
frictionless tube
delivers me
into the flesh
brainless smooth
agnostic want
before the flash</pre>

<p class="baker-title">Wet Bulb Temperature</p>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--baker">record heat
every year
supersedes
new frontiers
humid peaks
sharp as knives
fluid packs
won’t save lives
clinging sweat
organs roast
before long
deadly threat
only hope
freon god</pre>
