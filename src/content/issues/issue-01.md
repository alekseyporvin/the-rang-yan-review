editorial: "This inaugural issue of The Rangzen Review asks a foundational question: what is the relationship between the words we use and the work we do? Across essays, fiction, poetry, and critical notes, our contributors approach this question from different angles — historical, philosophical, personal. They do not arrive at a single answer. We are not sure a single answer exists. But we believe the question is worth asking carefully, and that the asking itself is a form of work. We are grateful to our contributors for the seriousness and generosity with which they have engaged this first issue, and to our readers for the trust they extend by opening these pages."
