shortBio: "Ghadah Kamal is a translator, writer, poet, visual artist, and musician whose work centers on surrealist literature and the visual arts in Egypt and the Middle East. She is co-founder and editorial director of Sulfur Editions, a founding member of the Middle East and North Africa Surrealist Group, and a co-founding member of the Chrysopoeia Surrealist Union."
fullBio: "She edits the Surrealist Cities section of *The Room* and the Sulfur Surrealist Jungle platform, and is publishing and events director and treasurer of the International Cooperative for Arts, Sciences and Technology (I COAST). With Mohsen Elblasy she published *The Goddess of the Moon*, surrealist texts and poems (Dar Badael, Egypt, 2018), and *The Wolves of the Moon*, poetry and collages in English, French, and Spanish (La Belle Inutile, 2021). Her other books include *The Man Who Ate the Watch* (Sefsafa, Egypt) and *The Last Summer of Mr. Dipodoidea*. Her translations include *The Man Who Decided to Become a Tree* by Yoyo, *Being on the Brink* by Ahmad Klij, and *Surplus Skin* by Ibrahim Abdelfattah. Her work also appears in *Oblivion*, *Revue de la poésie in toto*, *Lithaire 3*, and *Honidi*.\n\nKamal coordinated the International Exhibition of Surrealism (Cairo / Saint-Cirq-Lapopie, 2022), *Echoes of Contemporary Surrealism* (Alexandria and Budapest), and the Luxor Surrealist Symposium. She also organized *Échos Surréalistes Contemporains*, a touring project across Luxor, Saint-Cirq-Lapopie, Budapest, and Alabama."
