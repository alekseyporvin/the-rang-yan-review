name: "Cavalli, Guido"
shortBio: "Guido Cavalli (Parma, 1974) is an author and editor at the philosophy journal *Kasparhauser* ([www.kasparhauser.it](https://www.kasparhauser.it)). He has published three collections of poetry: *Piccolo canzoniere selvatico* (Manni Editori, 2005), *Nel castagneto* (with preface by Claudio Risé and afterword by Giovanni Ronchini, Edizioni Diabasis, 2015), and *Salita al lago Padre* (with illustrations by Andrea Bovaia, Manni Editori, 2018). His work appears in magazines, anthologies, and miscellanies (LietoColle, Bertoni editore, Puntoacapo edizioni, Mup). He has published two novels (*Cielo di paese*, Mobydick, 2001; *Scaramuccia*∗ Mobydick, 2004) under the collective pseudonym Errico Malò, as well as several short stories for newspapers, magazines, and anthologies (Guanda, Mup, Aliberti). Guido Cavalli holds a Ph.D. in Pedagogy from the Catholic University of Milan; his research focuses on the city and processes of urban transformation and regeneration, as well as health and generative welfare. He studied musical composition at the Boito Conservatory in Parma and philosophy at Ca’ Foscari University in Venice. He works in communications for the Third Sector and serves as president of the *Magnete* Community Center in Milan ([www.magnete.mi](https://www.magnete.mi))."
