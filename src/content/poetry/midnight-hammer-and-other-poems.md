**Midnight Hammer**

<div class="poem-verse">
<p>Refreshing and updating fails to untangle<br />
all the knots of truth and untruth that dominate<br />
and make up the so-called roots of this news cycle</p>
<p>All would-be compasses and novel projections<br />
keep collapsing, no attempt at measurements<br />
lasts for long on the bloodied floor of our horizon</p>
<p>There are rumors of destruction, hints of a peace,<br />
the warmongers pretend it is not a surrender,<br />
what are we supposed to grasp onto for direction?</p>
<p>Cute headlines keep struggling beside the atrocities,<br />
they leave potholes on the path to understanding,<br />
cats fumble with toys as the missiles fall</p>
</div>

**Sizing Up Utopia**

<div class="poem-verse">
<p>Going down to the local quad,<br />
I hear they're giving away some democracy today</p>
<p>This time they'll let me rank,<br />
A way to mix and match to scratch off a winner</p>
<p>Arrows in an archipelago guide<br />
To a gym with a ceiling pockmarked from dodgeballs</p>
<p>First come, most served,<br />
I've got all my cards in order, it's time for checks</p>
<p>Find the right names, create<br />
Some new ones in case I don't like what's offered</p>
<p>Maybe I can flip a coin or roll<br />
Some dice when no one is walking behind my booth</p>
<p>Part of a chance to feel powerful<br />
Like I'm the one moving things around the track</p>
</div>

**The Starter Cord**

<div class="poem-verse">
<p>What about lawnmower repair? There might be a future in that,<br />
the grass keeps growing, for now, and the blades<br />
get dull at some point, not to mention the screws get loose<br />
and the wires pull themselves out, as if by magic</p>
<p>Nothing too big to work on, like an automobile, or an autobus,<br />
and if I get the parts wrong over overlook an issue,<br />
nobody will die, maybe there will be a spark or a little flame,<br />
but what will fall out of the sky because I'm hungover?</p>
<p>It'll help with selling my reputation in the arts, being known<br />
as the "lawnmower repair poet," someone writing<br />
in their spare time between sorting and cleaning their spare parts,<br />
future interviewers will get to compare it to reaping</p>
</div>

**Larking in a Cafe**

<div class="poem-verse">
<p>In the absence of a reverie worth remembering,<br />
I take a book on symbolism off the shelf</p>
<p>The images might give my future sleeping self<br />
inspiration through uptake and processing</p>
<p>It does not matter what scenarios will unfold,<br />
only how distinct the picture show goes</p>
<p>A drama could emerge out of this medieval egg,<br />
or these roots painted by Vincent Van Gogh</p>
<p>And this stone relief of an ancient Mauryan owl<br />
will certainly send my subconscious shivering</p>
<p>As I flip along, I bat away silk ribbons, a rainbow<br />
of bookmarks attached to this thick volume</p>
<p>Each one sticks out like a shimmering tongue,<br />
fine for a simile, a terrible source for a dream</p>
</div>

**Into the Arena**

<div class="poem-verse">
<p>Old vineyards of hands and chatter are falling into decay,<br />
recommendations come, no body follows inside,<br />
sing, fellow kids, where's the party at anymore these days?</p>
<p>Booths are being pulled out, tables and chairs amputated,<br />
tendrils fill the dancefloors, online groups<br />
wind down into forums for special offers from local singles</p>
<p>Outlines flicker between burning bulbs and icy walls<br />
as the modified release of scrolling<br />
dissolves the free time otherwise too painful to pass inside</p>
<p>Maybe it is just me, names flock, faces bounce along<br />
but never the twain do any of them meet<br />
for today's prized threesomes that we once called contact</p>
<p>Still, I wait with society's nosebleeds, squeezing a program<br />
and waiting for a human to enter, stage right,<br />
who brings a security without me passing it on as rent</p>
</div>
