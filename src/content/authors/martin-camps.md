---
name: "Camps, Martín"
shortBio: "Mexican poet and translator (b. 1974). His most recent collections of poetry include *Los días baldíos*, *La invención del mundo*, and *Crónica del incendio de los días*. His articles, poems, and translations have appeared in *Círculo de Poesía*, *Bitter Oleander*, *Tierra Adentro*, *Modern Poetry in Translation*, *Reverso*, *Revista de literatura mexicana contemporánea*, *Huizache*, and the cultural supplements to Mexico’s major newspapers. He recently published his translation into Spanish of *Parque Industrial: Novela Proletaria* by Brazilian author Patrícia Galvão."
