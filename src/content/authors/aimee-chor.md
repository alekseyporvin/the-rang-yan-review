name: "Chor, Aimee"
shortBio: "Poet and translator in Seattle. She studied religion at the University of Chicago and the universities of Tübingen and Munich, and has received DAAD, Mellon, and Fulbright fellowships. Her translations have appeared in *The Paris Review*, *Modern Poetry in Translation*, and other journals, and in *The Opposite of Seduction: New German Poetry* (2025)."
