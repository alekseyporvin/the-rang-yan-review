**Herself**

She wielded what she hoped would be her children's  
vulnerability, wheezed near weeds disguised  
as her good intent. She practiced seeming  
thoughtful, better described as ought-ful only  
slightly to the right of what she thought her right  
to shuffle feelings of offspring who failed  
to bow to her varietal maternity  
foisted on each in elaborately  
different ways. Would-be gifts sifted through  
gritted teeth ripe to leave bitemarks anywhere  
exposed. A husband ago she in her own  
mind vouchsafed to tap the shoulders of each spoke  
of resultant appearing likeness spawned  
then shouldered unwanted for requisite decades

**Ode to a Utility Infielder**

Handsome utility infielder  
dependably won't draw a crowd.  
Stands unthanked as a father,  
sturdy, always there,

picking up tasks: shortstop,  
the forgotten second base, third,  
equally fluent in all. Fans fill the stands  
to watch the starting pitcher,

perhaps the outfielder,  
while this handyman  
like a Buddha  
unhands any tendency

to worship one MVP  
as if a god, as if one  
might confuse oneself  
with such a deity.

**Parting Such Orderly Sweet**

We keep a good long melody in the glove compartment to preserve the apartment from littery things lacking any semblance of orderliness. He was just an orderly you decried for someone's not being a doctor. I protested out of ignorance, starting at the top with the name middled on parchment with thin signature beneath. I believed in him despite your relieving me of the duty of love. And about that enclosure: it's as unrosy as compost and just that hopeful. Hope lies upon the staff where whiffs of notes momentarily reside. I'm listening, as the call-in shrink would say to the air full of audience that sustained his paycheck, reruns yielding royalties. Speaking of which, keep singing unaccompanied, for I have always loved your voice. Reminds of eastern screech owl from the oak trees at night in the woods where we lived. Nostalgia lacks facthood in the neighborhood of wooden fences punched together to deflect intrusion. Meanwhile, I small to the task of brushing away unlikely forays into repair work best performed by solo acts claiming to love their work alongside a lack of tact.

**from Nebula #46**

In whose image would you say film noir  
caves to serotonin reuptake  
inhibition? Would you prefer the down  
days to play out? And do they know how to play?

The gas light gone extinct has blended into  
that one word. Can you prove how it unbottled  
you? Emptied self-trust to rust matching autumn  
leaves already fallen early in the night?

What is it about black and white that draws you?  
The folds of the clothing undisturbed by  
color? The way the fabric falls from  
the body, just far enough away to

distract from the animal within each of  
us that allows the eyes to inspect  
the threat perhaps inevitable  
we teach ourselves to learn may be true?

**Evening | Toned | Wings**

Her evening reverie  
a bone-toned  
whisper of rosary

not the brown wood  
that seeds hegemony  
of Medjugorie

closer to swallows  
rising with moon-  
shaped wings firming

their blend that swerves  
a mirror prayer  
to her own

beads closed in  
a louvered  
drawer
