name: "Baker, D.W."
shortBio: "D.W. Baker is a poet from St. Petersburg, Florida. His poems appear in *The Cincinnati Review*, *Sundog Lit*, *ballast*, and *dadakuku*, among others, and have received nominations for Best of the Net and the Pushcart Prize. His reviews and essays appear in *Version (9) Magazine*, *Philly Poetry Chapbook Review*, *A River Runs Through It*, and more. He is the author of *Love’s the Burning Boy* and co-author of *(e)laments*. See more of his work at [www.dwbakerpoetry.com](http://www.dwbakerpoetry.com)."
location: "St. Petersburg, Florida"
