name: "Armantrout, Rae"
shortBio: "Rae Armantrout was born in Vallejo, California. She studied at the University of California, Berkeley, with Denise Levertov, and at San Francisco State University, and is one of the founding members of the West Coast Language poets. She is professor emerita of writing at the University of California, San Diego, where she taught for more than twenty years and directed the New Writing Series. She lives in Everett, Washington.

Her books of poetry include *Safe Rooms* (Wesleyan University Press, 2026), *Go Figure* (2024), *Finalists* (2022), *Conjure* (2020), *Wobble* (2018), a finalist for the National Book Award, *Entanglements* (2017), *Partly: New and Selected Poems* (2016), *Itself* (2015), and *Versed* (2009), which received the Pulitzer Prize and the National Book Critics Circle Award and was a finalist for the National Book Award. She is also the author of the memoir *True* (1998) and *Collected Prose* (Singing Horse Press, 2007)."
