---
name: "Quasha, George"
shortBio: "American poet, artist, and musician, born in White Plains, New York. With Susan Quasha he founded Station Hill Press in Barrytown. His books include *Somapoetics*, *Giving the Lily Back Her Hands*, and *Verbal Paradise*; *Genius Foci* and *Hilaritas Sublime* are preverb series made with her photographs. He has held fellowships from the National Endowment for the Arts and the Guggenheim Foundation."
