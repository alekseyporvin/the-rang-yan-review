name: "Báez, Frank"
shortBio: "Frank Báez (Dominican Republic, 1978). Long considered one of the leading poets of his generation, Báez won the coveted Salomé Ureña Prize in 2009, and he has continued to publish widely in anthologies and journals; his full-length collections include *Este es el futuro que estabas esperando* (Seix Barral, 2017). In the same year of that collection’s publication, he was selected by the Hay Festival as a member of Bogotá39, the list of the best Latin American writers under forty years of age. His sense of humor, zest for life, fearlessness in melding pop culture with high culture, his fusion of spoken word energy with traditional verse, and his vision rooted in the *quisqueyano* experience make for a thrilling reading experience when discovering his poetry.\n\nIn 2022, Spuyten Duyvil published Seidman’s translation of *The End of the World Came to My Neighborhood*, with poems from that collection having been showcased in journals like Latin American Literature Today, Review: Literature and Arts of the Americas, and Acentos. The poems translated for this selection were culled from Báez’s most recent title, *Desarmando la biblioteca de mi padre* (Fondo de Cultura Económica, 2024)."
