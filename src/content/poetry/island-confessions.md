<p class="stein-title">ANGEL</p>
<p class="stein-place">San Francisco Bay</p>
<p class="stein-blank" aria-hidden="true"></p>
<p class="stein-voice">I’m no angel</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>Hisajiro Inouye and his family moved to America in 1916 and became farmers in San Jose </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">not by a long shot</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>was interned on Angel Island on August 14, 1943</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">I was christened <em>Isla de los Ángeles</em></p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>Don Juan de Ayala made a survey of the bay of San Francisco, for missions that were soon to be founded</em> </p>
<p class="stein-voice">mission accomplished    on to the next</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>federal roundup of Hindus illegally in this country was underway today to be taken to Angel Island for deportation</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">I confess    </p>
<p class="stein-voice">beware of so-named angels </p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>fought a duel at Angel Island on Saturday </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">beware sirens in the waters    </p>
<p class="stein-voice">they’re often garbed in angel-wear</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>On Angel Island, the introduction of hypnotism into treatment is due to wide learning of its physician </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">beware the heavenly    </p>
<p class="stein-voice">what goes on in the heavens </p>
<p class="stein-voice">can be hell</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>occupied Angel Island to construct bunkers for Nike missiles </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">be aware.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-stein">
<p class="stein-title">BEAVER</p>
<p class="stein-place">Lake Michigan</p>
<p class="stein-blank" aria-hidden="true"></p>
<p class="stein-voice">I confess    </p>
<p class="stein-voice">I am a siren</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>told of comforts to be found on Beaver Island</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">alluring</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>grub was nowhere so good, the liquors so plentiful, and the women so partial to sailors</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">I am a distant flame upon water</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>swept clean by a forest fire, all the inhabitants probably burned to</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">a rumored flame</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>false report that Beaver Island has been devastated by fire </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">a flame of lore</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>who moved to Beaver Island was a Russian nobleman who freed his serfs, which did not meet with the approval of the Czar </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">here you are free </p>
<p class="stein-voice">to be what you want</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>had a revelation wherein their future prosperity would be planted at Beaver Island</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">here you are free </p>
<p class="stein-voice">to believe what you want</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>Mormon kingdom ruled by James Strang </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">legends lived here</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>seating himself, King James asked where my father was </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">here there is a hidden treasure of legends</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>his custom to lie in wait for vessels, plunder them and make their crews remain and convert to their faith or else </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">if and where I will not confess</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>of supposed credibility have drawn grotesquely inaccurate pictures </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">here truth is hidden in rumor and lore</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>fired a revolver striking Strang in the head, the other a pistol at the small of his back</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">here your every maybe is free</p>
<p class="stein-archive stein-archive--short"><em>had been Mormons but </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">or here it may be</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>by a force of state officers </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">it matters not    </p>
<p class="stein-voice">come see that which you wish</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>Mormon buildings still standing </em> </p>
<p class="stein-voice">you too can inhabit rumor and lore</p>
<p class="stein-voice">come</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>one of the new family plans, Beaver Island now has the King Strang Hotel</em>  </p>
<p class="stein-voice">islands are sirens of rumor and lore.</p>
</div>

<p class="stein-title">NI’IHAU</p>
<p class="stein-place">Hawaii, Privately Owned</p>
<p class="stein-blank" aria-hidden="true"></p>
<p class="stein-voice">I am forbidden</p>
<p class="stein-voice"><em>noli me tangere</em>, for the Robinson’s I am </p>
<p class="stein-voice">do not think by this I mean </p>
<p class="stein-voice">Walt Disney’s Swiss family</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>owned by the Robinson estate, no outsider may visit without permission</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">still</p>
<p class="stein-voice">disconnection cannot exist </p>
<p class="stein-voice">without connection</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>when Kamehameha V sold Ni’ihau to Mrs. Sinclair for a ridiculously low price</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">and the continuance of connection </p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>my regard for the Robinson family extends to their grand-mother, Mrs. Sinclair, for her patriotic attentions to my chiefs </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">our majesties are gone</p>
<p class="stein-voice">still I remain</p>
<p class="stein-voice">alone   </p>
<p class="stein-voice">known only as </p>
<p class="stein-voice">Mystery Island   </p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>so isolated carrier pigeons bear messages to </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">when you think of me </p>
<p class="stein-voice">think of the many meanings </p>
<p class="stein-voice">of mystery</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>only a little cash was stolen, $8,150 being left </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">because I am forbidden</p>
<p class="stein-voice">little is known of me </p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>sad to think how very few can view its beauty</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">because I am forbidden</p>
<p class="stein-voice">little is known of you</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>didn’t even know that the United States and Japan were at war</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">because I am forbidden</p>
<p class="stein-voice">hidden here is fear</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>Japanese fighter pilot who crash landed on the island single-handedly captured it with his machine gun</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">still</p>
<p class="stein-voice">when you think of me</p>
<p class="stein-voice">think of a majestic age</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>even the little island of Ni’ihau had her giants</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">think of majestic lore</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>hurled his spear so furiously at the Kauai giant that the impact of the spear left a great hole which may still be seen </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">think of the many meanings </p>
<p class="stein-voice">of meaning</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>uninjured, Limaloa wrenched a rock off the cliff near Polihale and hurled it twenty miles at the Ni’ihau giant </em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">think of the meanings of </p>
<p class="stein-voice">the mind’s imaginings</p>
<p class="stein-archive"><em>to this day, it is called, Pohaku o Kamaile</em>, <em>Giant’s Rock</em></p>
<p class="stein-voice">look at me and see </p>
<p class="stein-voice">the mind’s imagined reality. </p>
