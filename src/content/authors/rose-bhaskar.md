shortBio: "Rose Bhaskar spends her life as a writer, exploring how that calling operates within higher education, industry, and politics in a globalizing world. She is a researcher by training, holding two graduate degrees grounded in qualitative research and international practice. She continues education out of a constant curiosity about how the status quo came to be and whether it is permanent. As a freelance writer, she has worked across industries with small businesses, educational institutions, executives, and creatives who share a need to turn complicated material into clear, well-made writing. What interests Rose in any project is the chance to write a good story — one that bridges perspectives and provokes thought. Her priorities as a creative are truth, voice, and aesthetic. You can find Rose featured across several digital and print literary magazines, or explore [Rose Writes Stuff](https://rosewritesstuff.substack.com/), her Substack publication housing personal essays and political commentary. She is at work on a book-length project and is open to consultations."
