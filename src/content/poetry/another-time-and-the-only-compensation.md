<p><strong>Another Time</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">This is the unique revolt
that doesn’t understand a thing, delays
to be present, sizes up
and says, “Look, I made
time last”
but time is a story, an unjust story, the joy
that arrived late
now that things don’t hide
and the riverbed is no longer a secret
everything is certain now
it’s there, unique, too unique to be present
and yet this meadow really exists--
these low tufts, even if there’s no measurement.

Every time boats
cross the water,
segments that demand their circle
and someone asks who
have you become, who
do you love now,
and feels the same, or would like to . . . 
but repeats the words
for never, their crepuscular violence
which sees everything, but finds nothing
lukewarm soup shared at the same table
cooled by the ignorance
of those who drink not to waste, and waste everything
even the defeat
sung by another, always
by another, who takes their place, mercifully
and tries to live: today
words really don’t echo off the wall
that protected them, in a bet won
because abandoned early: today,
for the first time
they wanted to triumph.</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>“The only compensation for your loss? Being unable to know its exact limits”</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">“Shut up. You only talk to forget.”
There were no walls
and phrases disappeared in the wind.
“You know my name
signifies: I’ve changed”
and then there’s no silence, she says, if you realize,
there’s no love
at the end of a memory.
The shadow of the houses has disappeared
in this exterior of trucks and lime
and the space is too much
for words to be there.
“You didn’t invent the joy, you’re not
smart enough . . . you despair
because what you do becomes . . . ”
And these trees, increasingly sparse
the wide roads in the northern part of the city
fog covering everything, footsteps
“But you can’t go on like this for long . . . ”
and hands are damp, like the grass, beyond the road
still, they don’t touch, they’ll never be
sentimental.
“All you’ve done is waste time. Start,
for once,
with what you’ve got left.”</pre>
