shortBio: "Clara Burghelea is a poet, literary translator, and educator whose work moves between poetry, translation, and cross-cultural literary exchange. She is the author of two poetry collections, *The Flavor of the Other* (Dos Madres Press, 2020) and *Praise the Unburied* (Chaffinch Press, 2021), as well as *The Clear Sky* (Dos Madres Press, 2025), her first collection of poetry in translation. Her writing and translation practice reflect a sustained engagement with the ways language, memory, and cultural identity shape literary expression across borders."
fullBio: "She holds an MFA from Adelphi University in New York and is a PhD candidate in Literature at the University of Texas at Dallas. As Literary Editor for Romanian Literature at *The Rang Yan Review*, Burghelea contributes to the journal’s engagement with contemporary Romanian writing, bringing her experience as a poet, translator, and scholar to the discovery and presentation of literary voices from Romania and its wider cultural sphere."
