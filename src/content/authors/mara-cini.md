---
name: "Cini, Mara"
shortBio: "An Italian artist whose practice encompasses writing in its manifold forms, she has collaborated with influential experimental journals, including *Tam Tam*, and currently serves as editor of *Anterem Edizioni*. She has participated in poetry readings and literary events, including *Romapoesia* and the Venice Biennale. Her publications include *Scritture* (1979), *La direzione della sosta* (1982), *Anni e altri riti* (1987), *Dentro Fuori Casa* (1995), and *Monte Sole* (2024). Her prose works include *Specchio convesso* (2006), co-authored with Rita Degli Esposti, and a short story featured in Gianni Celati’s *Narratori delle riserve* (1992)."
