**Portrait of Thelonious Monk as a Puritan in a Haptic Capotain**

Now you tell me something I need to know where am I  
supposed to find a rhythm section at this late  
date in this second rate burg that reads  
standards as if they were a floating bridge  
when your standup bass stand you up and cymbals  
snare another gig that pay beyond  
union wage for a rising standup  
such as Flip Wilson or Red Foxx  
especially when you need a hit  
any kind of hit will do pink  
sea salt a record  
amount of Thorazine  
a hit rock record please  
mister explain how something so simple  
as lacking seasoning make me sweat this much while I stride  
like Turkish taffy to the distaff side  
would carrot juice rebalance chemicals  
Vitamin Doc injects them in my veins  
but tone deaf neighbor in 4F ban the buzz of Nelli’s juicer  
crunch of a mere celery stalk  
out thump Monk’s stringy Kimball upright  
why threaten  
to confiscate my cabaret card  
for mumbling while I tinkle

and why you want to make me sweat  
when I already bear the burdens of a family man  
searching half my whole adult life for a quarter  
note between notes I scored way back  
when forming long fingers imperfectly informed  
for a mere three Gauloises or a button  
not as your redeemer  
and certainly not as a visitor rather than a refugee  
seeking asylum at this late date  
because like the storm of history blowing  
wreckage in my face  
jazz is never getting any better never  
can ever get any better never ever  
so why not allow me this shuffle beside a nostalgic melancholic  
who claims to be the replacement bassist in turtle shell shades  
searching through my suitcase for scales  
while all I ever really wanted to score is a room  
service mess of liver and onions and mashed potatoes and gravy while I enjoy  
*The Price is Right* in bed and play dead in pressed pinstripe  
contemplating removing every single one of my legendary chapeaus  
apart from a haptic capotain to signify  
I lay me down as if I were a church of one.
