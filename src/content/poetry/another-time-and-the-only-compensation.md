<p><strong>Selections from <em>Resemblances</em> (1976)</strong></p>

<p><strong>A Generosity</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">This is a dance
where steps aren’t learned
for it to be (“I’ll take
everything by surprise”) and it doesn’t resign itself
to halfhearted talk, now that the wind
carries the smell of ploughed earth
and shouts out things
as if they existed
and they come (“See, you don’t have to choose
anymore”) toward the splendor
of swimming, an improvised
fate, which reveals itself later
in the thick of the current
but in silence,
silence, testing.</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>On the Point</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">Now that I speak of you
without naming you . . . only now . . . 
beyond analysis . . . where nothing is
whole . . . but acts . . . 
I say . . . try . . . from the beginning . . . 
don’t fix . . . don’t figure on anybody . . . 
others can’t sustain
an embrace . . . try . . . 
don’t negotiate . . . don’t prepare . . .
don’t last . . .  </pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>Largo Afternoon</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">Now canoes are crossing the river
movement
in exuberant sunlight, and looking
shuts down reason

where this death is not simply a fading away
but being too, briefly,
on the edge of joy
which opens and lightly bears
an offer in the glimmer of a drop
and comment is excluded
when river banks in the morning
bring their strength
messenger of a name, listening,
conveying the body’s will, the imminent caress

<span class="deangelis-in">looking         living         here</span>

the intact season
has time to last
but pushes beyond
without rummaging through rubble
and calls for writing
that isn’t noticeable.</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>Where Everything Is Connected</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">Being called,
it’s never dark here,
it’s always later, amid
obligations, on trams, sinking into coats
with things that need to be finished--everything.
And even now rain
on glossy windows
can’t be nature or history
but an episode
that every winter knows how to repeat
living and circular
while everything demanded a different attitude
which believes in each thing
without second-guessing it, a lightness in the cells,
a smile in the right place . . . 

. . . intensities, only viscous intensities
between mother and mamma’s voice
like these sidewalks
that try to divide
but join with the cars
and this vice
of recognizing, and its commands,
voice useless, in the midst of a storm,
which is kissed,
kissed . . . kissed . . . 

Via Pacini. It’s raining, heavier and heavier.
Someone asked me the time</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>Another Time</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">This is the unique revolt
that doesn’t understand a thing, delays
to be present, sizes up
and says, “Look, I made
time last”
but time is a story, an unjust story, the joy
that arrived late
now that things don’t hide
and the riverbed is no longer a secret
everything is certain now
it’s there, unique, too unique to be present
and yet this meadow really exists--
these low tufts, even if there’s no measurement.

Every time boats
cross the water,
segments that demand their circle
and someone asks who
have you become, who
do you love now,
and feels the same, or would like to . . . 
but repeats the words
for never, their crepuscular violence
which sees everything, but finds nothing
lukewarm soup shared at the same table
cooled by the ignorance
of those who drink not to waste, and waste everything
even the defeat
sung by another, always
by another, who takes their place, mercifully
and tries to live: today
words really don’t echo off the wall
that protected them, in a bet won
because abandoned early: today,
for the first time
they wanted to triumph.</pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>“The only compensation for your loss? Being unable to know its exact limits”</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">“Shut up. You only talk to forget.”
There were no walls
and phrases disappeared in the wind.
“You know my name
signifies: I’ve changed”
and then there’s no silence, she says, if you realize,
there’s no love
at the end of a memory.
The shadow of the houses has disappeared
in this exterior of trucks and lime
and the space is too much
for words to be there.
“You didn’t invent the joy, you’re not
smart enough . . . you despair
because what you do becomes . . . ”
And these trees, increasingly sparse
the wide roads in the northern part of the city
fog covering everything, footsteps
“But you can’t go on like this for long . . . ”
and hands are damp, like the grass, beyond the road
still, they don’t touch, they’ll never be
sentimental.
“All you’ve done is waste time. Start,
for once,
with what you’ve got left.”</pre>
