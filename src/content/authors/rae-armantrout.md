name: "Armantrout, Rae"
shortBio: "Rae Armantrout is a Pulitzer Prize-winning poet and one of the founding members of the West Coast Language poetry movement. She is the author of more than twenty books of poetry, including *Safe Rooms* (2026), *Go Figure* (2024), *Finalists* (2022), *Conjure* (2020), *Wobble* (2018), a finalist for the National Book Award, *Partly: New and Selected Poems* (2016), *Itself* (2015), and *Versed* (2009), which received the Pulitzer Prize and the National Book Critics Circle Award and was a finalist for the National Book Award. Her poetry has appeared in numerous literary journals and major anthologies, including *The New Yorker*, *The Paris Review*, *Poetry*, *The Best American Poetry*, and *The Norton Anthology of Postmodern American Poetry*. She is also the author of the memoir *True* (1998) and *Collected Prose* (2007). Armantrout is Professor Emerita of Writing at the University of California, San Diego, where she taught for more than twenty years and directed the New Writing Series for nearly two decades."
