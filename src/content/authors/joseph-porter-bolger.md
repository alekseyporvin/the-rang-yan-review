---
name: "Porter-Bolger, Joseph"
shortBio: "Joseph Porter-Bolger is a poet and interdisciplinary artist based in Peterborough, whose work draws on opera, musicology, and his experience as an unpaid carer. His writing has appeared or is forthcoming in Dodging the Rain, Amethyst Review, and Puck Press. His spoken-word soundscape *An Introduction to Apoplexy* received Arts Council England funding for further development. He has also written the libretto for Arne Gieshoff’s opera *The Viagron*, performed at the Tête-à-Tête and Grimeborn festivals. Holding a PhD from King’s College London, his academic interests include the influence of seventeenth-century metaphysical poetry on modernism and the poetic representation of agony."
