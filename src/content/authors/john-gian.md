name: "Gian, John"
shortBio: "John Gian is an Italian poet, visual artist, performer, and translator based in Venice. Born in Vicenza in 1949, he has been active in international experimental poetry and visual arts since the 1970s. His work has been presented at numerous exhibitions and international events, including several editions of the Venice Biennale. He is the author of numerous poetry collections and collaborative publications, and has translated works by prominent American poets, including Robert Creeley, Ted Berrigan, Philip Whalen, and Anne Waldman. His artistic practice explores the intersections of visual poetry, experimental writing, sound, and performance."
