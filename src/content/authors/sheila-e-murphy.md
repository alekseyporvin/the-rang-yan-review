name: "Murphy, Sheila E."
shortBio: "A Pushcart-nominated poet, recent appearances include *Gargoyle*, *Poetry Bay*, *Poetry*, *Lana Turner*, *Poetose*, *Volta*, and others. Most recent book publications are *I Want to Be Your Radio* (Unlikely Books, 2025), *Escritoire* (Lavender Ink, 2025), and *Permission to Relax* (BlazeVOX Books, 2023). Recently performed at Piper Writers Center Arizona State University and the New Orleans Poetry Festival. Won the Gertrude Stein Poetry Award for *Letters to Unfinished J.* (Green Integer Press, 2003) and the Hay(ha)ku Book Prize for *Reporting Live From You Know Where* (Meritage Press, 2018). She lives in Phoenix, Arizona."
