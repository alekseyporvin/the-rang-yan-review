name: "Cardenas, J.N."
shortBio: "J.N. Cardenas is a poet, filmmaker, and literary translator from Los Angeles, with additional roots in Bolivia. His work has been published in such journals as Entropy, Sulfur Surrealist Jungle, Círculo de Poesía (Mexico), and Párrafo Magazine (UCLA). His translation of the long poem *Yelidá* by Dominican poet Tomás Hernández Franco (co-translated with Anthony Seidman) was published by Trainwreck Press of Canada. He is currently working on a collection of short stories and poems, and a documentary about poets from Mexico’s northern border region."
