<p><strong>1</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">after work and shopping
back home watch news on tv


ants can’t almost make it anymore
flushed out from the last flours
live out of toothpaste crumbs
left in the bathroom


we review history observing
the past live
right before our eyes


beaten dog in the hail
control launches its top offensive
somewhere
out of a blue egg
a different kind of being comes to light



for Dadi’s agenda</pre>

<pre class="poem-lines">at daylight
not finding

<p><strong>3</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">150 hours

my father would splay blue pasta wrappings
over the bombed out
windows

AKIRA KUROSAWA

chickens were our toys they let themselves be
swooped up in our arms like kittens

in the sky a white plane passed the grass was
green the sky blue it was spring like

(in the 7th month it became clear she
expected a son)

(the solitude weighed on her heart
like morning mist on peaks)</pre>

<hr>

<p><strong>4</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines">exercise #2

to get into
the world of my
cookie jar

into the silence
of prunes
heavy
with snow

for Cid Corman</pre>
