**XXXVIII**

<div class="poem-verse">
<p>This crystal waits to be sipped<br />
in the rough by an upcoming mouth<br />
without teeth. Not toothless.<br />
This crystal is bread not yet arrived.</p>
<p>It hurts when they force it<br />
and no longer has animal affections.<br />
But if impassioned, it would honey<br />
and take the mould of substantives<br />
that adjectivize on offering themselves.</p>
<p>Those who see it there a colourless<br />
sad individual, would send it off for love,<br />
for the past and at most for the future:<br />
if it is not itself given by any of its sides;<br />
if it waits to be sipped at once<br />
and as transparency, by an up-<br />
coming mouth that will no longer have teeth.</p>
<p>This crystal has passed from animal<br />
and now off it goes to form lefts,<br />
the new Minuses.</p>
<p>Just leave it alone.</p>
</div>

**XXXIX**

<div class="poem-verse">
<p>Who's lit a match!<br />
Tear me hair. I smile<br />
to swing by reason.<br />
I smile even more, if everyone comes<br />
to see the colourless guides<br />
and to me always punctual. Why should I care.</p>
<p>Not even that good Sun who, dying of pleasure,<br />
divvies everything up to distribute it<br />
among shadows, the prodigal,<br />
nor would he wait for me on the other side.<br />
Nor the others who only stop by<br />
entering and leaving.</p>
<p>The great baker calls<br />
with ringing retinas. And we pay in gestures<br />
most curious the warm undeniable value<br />
baked, transcendent.<br />
And we take our coffee, already late,<br />
with insufficient sugar that's been lacking,<br />
and butterless bread. What can one do?</p>
<p>But, yes, the rings reinforced, barred.<br />
Health goes on one foot. Forward: march!</p>
</div>

**XL**

<div class="poem-verse">
<p>Who would have told us that on a Sunday<br />
like this, on arachnid slopes<br />
the shadow would rear-up as pure frontal.</p>
<p>(A mollusk attacks barren salt-scoured eyes,<br />
roughly two or more tantalising chances<br />
against a half death-rattle of racked blood).</p>
<p>Then, not even the reverse of the uninhabited<br />
screen would dry up the arteries<br />
extradosed with double yet-to-bes.</p>
<p>As if they would have let us leave! As<br />
if we weren't always fæðmed<br />
with the two daily flanks of fatality!</p>
<p>And how much we'd have been offended.<br />
And yet how much we'd have annoyed each other<br />
and fought and made up again<br />
and again.</p>
<p>Who would have thought up such a Sunday,<br />
when, crawling, six elbows sup<br />
in this way, Mondayish sterile yolks.</p>
<p>We would have plucked against it, from under<br />
the two wings of Love,<br />
lustrous third feathers, poniards,<br />
new passages on eastern paper.</p>
<p>For today when we prove that we're still living,<br />
almost no more than a front.</p>
</div>
