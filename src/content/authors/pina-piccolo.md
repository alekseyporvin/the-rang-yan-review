name: "Piccolo, Pina"
shortBio: "Italian American poet, translator, and cultural worker. Born in California and educated at the University of California, Berkeley (Ph.D. in Italian literature), she moved to Italy in 2003. She is a co-founder of *La Macchina Sognante* and editor of *The Dreaming Machine*. Her poetry collection *I canti dell’Interregno* was published in 2018."
