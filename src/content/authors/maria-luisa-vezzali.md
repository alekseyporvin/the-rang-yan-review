name: "Vezzali, Maria Luisa"
shortBio: "Italian poet, teacher, and translator (b. 1964, Bologna). She teaches literature in secondary school. Her books include *lineamadre* (Donzelli, 2007; Anterem/Montano Prize), *Tutto questo* (puntoacapo, 2018; Don Luigi Di Liegro Prize), and *Lo spettro di casa* (puntoacapo, 2025). She has translated Adrienne Rich and Lorand Gaspar."
