<pre class="poem-lines">*
about the moon looking

about looking through the windows
to look at the voice

*
water is boiling
steam is writing
she puts her heart in the air

*
no assonance

doubting
under each syllable

at a certain point
on the pavement

alive

*
in the middle of silence
she looses the pearl
entirely closing in it

*
each letter
shouting to return

a little further
the blade touches us

*
waiting for
the leaf smelling of burning
confidant of just waiting
all crumbly

*
the warm disagreement
of hours without fever

gestures leaning forward

where has he been tomorrow
all of a jealousy

*
sleep noise

secret profile
of breaking
mixture of dreams

*
fate returns unexpected
in the race

on one side the flour
and the bread

on one side she
her impermanence</pre>
