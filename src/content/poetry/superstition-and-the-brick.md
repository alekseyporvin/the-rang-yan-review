**Superstition**

<div class="poem-verse">
<p>I feel deliberate w/ neon sickness<br />
only deliverable w/ slights suffered<br />
& small steps under the sun</p>
<p>I craft a mirage of movement<br />
in the heat & my eyes lose focus<br />
only wither is left on rebound</p>
<p>All of our curses are humid tests<br />
& the wilt is professionalized<br />
into a household rotten husk</p>
<p>I bleed my cuticles like animals<br />
& weaponize my compulsions—<br />
stop me before I taste honey</p>
<p>A crate of bleach & cracked bulbs<br />
rides shotgun in a broken car—<br />
I love you is superstition</p>
<p>The density of a drive time<br />
hangover is measured by the volume<br />
of a memory mocking a traffic light</p>
<p>I can only pick up a phone & break<br />
silence w/ myself & absence<br />
I can only sing in a key that's gone</p>
</div>

**A BRICK**

<div class="poem-verse">
<p>I am spent like a brick<br />
& the silence retorts<br />
wrestle with my shadow</p>
<p>a tracking mechanism<br />
my tongue is on the verge<br />
of eviction from my mouth</p>
<p>a truculent infection<br />
knotted to my throat<br />
all blue shiver & shake</p>
<p>mourning means<br />
a dove is the best breaker<br />
of silence in gold light</p>
<p>& grace is the elusive<br />
syllable of mourning<br />
moving like an arabesque</p>
<p>grace you are a filament<br />
flickering in threat<br />
you are hope feeding famine</p>
</div>
