---
name: "Fink, Thomas"
shortBio: "American poet and literary critic (b. 1954), professor of English at LaGuardia Community College, City University of New York. His books of poetry include *Selected Poems & Poetic Series* (Marsh Hawk Press, 2016), *Zeugma* (Marsh Hawk Press, 2022), and *Or Current Resident* (Marsh Hawk Press, 2025). “Yinglish Strophes 9” appeared in *The Best American Poetry 2007*. His criticism includes *“A Different Sense of Power”*, and he edits the interview zine *Dichtung Yammer*."
