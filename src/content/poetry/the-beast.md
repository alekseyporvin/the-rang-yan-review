excerpt: "Three poems: The Beast and Plato’s Theory of Forms, After Reading George Orwell’s 1984, and The Beast Refutes the Power of Language. The Beast emerges as the grotesque subject of a totalizing rationality whose ultimate ambition is to abolish every limit between thought and power, language and domination, the historical record and the sovereign fantasy of its author. Through a savage détournement of Plato, Orwell, and Derrida, these poems expose the authoritarian impulse at the heart of systems that claim mastery over reality itself, revealing how the desire to monopolize truth culminates in a regime of absolute self-reference where even the machinery of domination devours its master."

<div class="poem-stubbs">
<p class="stubbs-yeats">‘And what rough beast, its hour come round at last,</p>
<p class="stubbs-yeats">Slouches towards Bethlehem to be born?’</p>
<p class="stubbs-yeats-attr"><span class="stubbs-dash">—</span>W. B. Yeats, <em>The Second Coming</em></p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-title">THE BEAST AND PLATO’S THEORY OF FORMS</p>
<p class="stubbs-right"><em>For Nicolas Cavaillès</em></p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-right"><em>‘In the knowledgeable realm, the form of the good is the last thing to be seen, and it is<br />reached with difficulty.</em></p>
<p class="stubbs-right">—Plato</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">On a rare day in which you feel less than</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">good, maladroit in fact, human-headed, and</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">unable to see life through:</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">we rediscover you, unsure of the circumstances</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">that have you still</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">slouching the world…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">hence, the reason why, in a library, you</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">begin to study it:</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">Plato’s Theory of Forms.</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-27">Thus, each dawn,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">we find you training</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">your non-self to reach that</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">higher, but parachuteless</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">terrain beyond the sky…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">But how exactly?</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">By becoming this:</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">an incalculably deft acrobat</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">who, for all eternity,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">flips up and catches</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">only <em>himself</em>…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">and/or a celestial magician, who pulls</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">God infinitely out</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">of a diaphanous hat!</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">that, or as a hypnotist</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">who tricks his own</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">soul into a trance of</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">cataleptic silence.</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">But what then when these improbable tasks have been accomplished?</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">Check for signs of vertigo</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">by holding a spirit-level containing Aristotle’s blood,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">while <em>not</em> listening out</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">for the thud of your body back on vertiginous matter…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-28">As you, grinning from ear</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">to ear, you get</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-27">ready finally to disappear…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-27">(to depart now every brain),</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-27">disguised as Plato garbed</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-27">in his shadowy rags of flesh…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-27">disguised as Plato garbed</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-27">as an Ideal Being<em>; </em>wearing</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-27">what you would not have been</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-27">seen dead in back on Earth.</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-title">AFTER READING GEORGE ORWELL’S 1984</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-right"><em>‘You asked once, what was in Room 101. I told you that you knew the answer already.<br />Everybody knows it. The thing that is in Room 101 is the worst thing in the world.’</em></p>
<p class="stubbs-right">—George Orwell</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">After reading 1984</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-33">you decide on only <em>this</em></p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-33">conclusion to untruth</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">in Oceania: God strapped and howling in a leather chair in Room 101,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">and you torturing him,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">there, where, with your new improved versions of <em>his</em> universe,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">you send in, subliminally,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">not rats, but <em>your</em> thoughts</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">through the<em> memory holes</em> inside <em>his</em> head, ‘thoughts’ to reset his definitions</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">until he can only see</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34"><em>you</em> in place of himself!</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">But for what purpose?</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">For the improvements of the arts, literature, philosophy,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">science, politics, media, and above all, religious education!</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">Turning <em>Newspeak</em> into</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">(simply) <em>Beastspeak</em>…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-19">by transmitting on the telescreens in every room</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-19">‘Five Easy Steps’ (for all lapsed megalomaniacs)</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">on how best to scrub their skin</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">of their last totalitarian sins…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">Until when in THE MINISTRY OF BEAST</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-19">a document is signed off by every beastly</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">paper-pusher that you, and you alone</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">are <em>all</em> there is, and all there (forever)</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-18">can be…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">to confirm (now) that from this day on in every zone in every city</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">2 + 2 will equal <em>Beast</em>, not 5!</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">And that (likewise) all historical</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">records will now be doctored</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">until it is confirmed that you</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">(and no one else)</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">began, won, and ended every human war;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">to ensure that all oligarchical activity is over…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">Up to the day when there is no one left in the universe to interrogate,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">and you, the beast, you have</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">no choice but to interrogate <em>yourself</em>!</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">(ripping out your own spine</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">to use now as a microphone)</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">To talk and talk and talk your</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">body into truth’s final corpse…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">until man’s last lies drop like</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-34">dead flies from the remains of it.</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-title">THE BEAST REFUTES THE POWER OF LANGUAGE</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-right"><em>‘I am the apocalypse itself, that is to say, the ultimate and first event of the end, the unveiling<br />and the verdict’</em></p>
<p class="stubbs-right">—Jacques Derrida</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">You refute it</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">by silencing grammarians</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">who believe that they</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">can still anatomise you,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">—but how exactly?</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">By prying free each name</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">as if a barnacle from every object</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">in the world, to dislodge all nouns, until expectorating (even?)</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">the final full-stop in the Bible</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">—but to ensure what?</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">That language itself is stripped</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">of its meaning: so that logocentrism now can</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">only be undone by <em>your</em> tongue!</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">To bring you to the metaleptic</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">conclusion of all idioms? Yes,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">to deconstruct deconstruction:</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">by cocking your ear to Christ’s</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">2000-year silence, to fossilise, at once, the holiest</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">tongues of all men in your palate…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">and by rolling, like a dung beetle,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">the last strips of landmass on Earth</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">into a ball to prevent even one</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">linguist from approaching you…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">to guarantee you remain the only</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">object to exist ‘<em>outside the text</em>’!</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">You, who (you conclude)</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">have become the first post-</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">grammatical anomaly to survive it, what? The</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">black death of meaninglessness!</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">which you celebrate</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">by ventriloquising Yeats’</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">jawbones back into speech,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">to shred instantaneously the last critical text?</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">yes, and to force (again?)</p>
<p class="stubbs-line"><em>yourself</em> into becoming</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">both signifier and signified…</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">because refusing to be compared with anything else!</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">You, who, having utterly</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">ended semiotics,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">feel free now to practice</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">your own set of new body signs in the mirror,</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">signs interpretable only to those</p>
<p class="stubbs-line">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">wearing <em>your</em> face mask on</p>
<p class="stubbs-line stubbs-in-30">the other side of reflection.</p>
