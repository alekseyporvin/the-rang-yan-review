---
name: "Mazer, Ben"
shortBio: "Ben Mazer is the author of eleven poetry collections, including *Selected Poems* (MadHat Press). He has edited the poetry of John Crowe Ransom, Delmore Schwartz, Landis Everson, Harry Crosby, and Frederick Goddard Tuckerman. His most recent edited book, with Blake Campbell, is *An Anthology of the Berkeley Renaissance* (MadHat Press)."
