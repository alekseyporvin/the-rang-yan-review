<pre class="poem-lines">Staggered out of a dream 
	toward the upstairs bathroom
Then I saw you
You were on the hallway floor,
		one arm flung ahead
	like you were swimming across the wood
You were still in your waiter’s uniform: 
 White shirt, black pants
It was three or four a.m. 
And I wondered if you were breathing
Five, six, seven, eight seconds
You didn’t breathe; I couldn’t—  
I lived in a future 
Without your legs laying across it</pre>
