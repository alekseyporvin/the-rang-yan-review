<p><strong><em>Variations on the Numerology of Home (ii)</em></strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--black">
But didn’t we inherit gusts of belief when the birds carried 
the incendiary in their beaks and dropped us
our tin bodies a contrivance 

build from an outhouse of language from the head wreck
as cherished as the Typhoon’s black doves in the bruised rain {                        } from the toothy 
world’s lurid complaint when you 
&#9;&#9;&#9;wrote another letter with forgotten postage and cracked paste
<em>fall liquid through the lurid pace</em> 
<em>&#9;&#9;illumine the imagined collapse into a home, </em>

{                        }

the opened parentheses we became in a new name<em> </em>for the same old collapse 
of dictum&#9;the reimagined squawk somnambulant in a baby’s throat 
our infant’s future stuck &#9;&#9;in the gullet of the drainpipe of an <em>unknown you </em>

once you bought a prophet a prayer as he washed&#9; the soles of his shoes 
with pothole sink stink&#9;&#9;&#9;the ink wavered away like a mirage 
sheep slip through a border and leaps&#9;&#9;
the spinning in the ditch broken &#9;&#9;the teeth removed for dentures pulled up at the root

we dance musky improvisations with newly mortgaged time as the darkness housed 
between the ribs grows grew-by-grew, <em>In den verfahrenen Augen*</em>
the middle of a sentence inextricably you, unbuckled

<em>at last</em> the begun in the middle of a learned pause {                            } <em>the simplest things las</em>t*<em> </em>

the theological future birthed in <em>centhropic</em> bursts of cat call’s claw
the last unborn<em> on </em>a mother’s tongue undresses 
&#9;&#9;&#9;&#9;&#9;the sky with button and bone, unpicking 

our <em>maternalstongue</em> meaning shovelled from the snow and black pine 
comb<em> &#9;&#9;</em>unbuckle your hair from unruly clumps of <em>flambeau </em>
<em>and hang it on the line to dry like mammalian hide</em>
<em>&#9;&#9;&#9;&#9;&#9;&#9;&#9;</em>you tried 

to unfasten your child’s desire from the predatory dull of stony things<em> in </em>the manner all dinosaur fought over each other, the chewed-up wild stone left for dead in the water-sogged grammar books and savings bank notes burned in a pile of bric-a-brac, <em>as bones bitch at the flame candle-up</em>

mother, you left me behind in a tobacco tin in the woods with the rabi’s disputes where contention broke through the trees in a full gallop, red stirrups and a black hood stood aside as belief stretched its spindly legs in an obsidian spurt
&#9;&#9;Birch cracks like the bones in your hands one <em>phalange </em>at a time

<em>blackmilkatsunbreak</em>, listen to the bereavement rattle like dried shells necklaced to our throats

all this for a prayer as we outran 
&#9;&#9;&#9;&#9;the perfidious sun
</pre>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--black">
i
The old questions run with the shadows flickering on the lawn
The same oneiric oration scampers through the branches with the squirrels
The matrimony in the trees pauses in a propitious laugh at the grave

ii 
<span class="black-num">1.</span>the lines in a poem branch into a rivulet of rain along from the valley of a leaf
<span class="black-num">2.</span>the back of an infant’s spine falls toward herbaceous forms of light
<span class="black-num">3.</span>the old questions’ propinquity speaks in the discarded digital mouth of the dead
<span class="black-num">4.</span>precipitation stalks the world in camera and a clamp-downed pen
<span class="black-num">5.</span>a hound bays in the back seat of a stolen car
<span class="black-num">6.</span>under a torn bedding we write books late into the morning
<span class="black-num">7.</span>a damp loquaciousness smothers the hound yelp for the fox
<span class="black-num">8.</span>a bewildered Romeo removes his epaulets and shiver under a banket
<span class="black-num">9.</span>a drunken raven covers the moon in window dressing found in the garden
<span class="black-num">10.</span>we bundle torn bags gravel and kibble in the back of a truck to carry patrimony
<span class="black-num">11.</span>a well’s rusty winch wobbles with insobriety in the mid-day sun
<span class="black-num">12.</span>a photograph of an affair ripens on the vine and falls purple and blue

<span class="black-num">13.</span>an owl haunts a misbegotten outhouse with words cleaned in a prospector’s pan 
<span class="black-num">14.</span>we weave a new set of teeth with oak branch and twigs and become indivisible
<span class="black-num">15.</span>we learn to whittle alchemical matrimony the size of winter peas
<span class="black-num">16.</span>put away your phone, put away your camera, put away your second skin
<span class="black-num">17.</span>divine death thirst huddled in the Aurum flicker of a tongue’s <em>tailsnap</em> 
<span class="black-num">18.</span>clean out the tins set to fire on the kitchen stove
<span class="black-num">19.</span>lose your heart near an intemperate creek
<span class="black-num">20.</span>bundle the debris of heirloom silver forgotten in the middle drawer 
<span class="black-num">21.</span>unstick your grandma’s desk stuck by an uncle’s efforts to unlock his story
<span class="black-num">22.</span>pick up a pen made from dragon’s eye and lotus bone and ink your wrist
<span class="black-num">23.</span>transcribe what <em>the seen</em> says to you in a dream, “<em>what next?”</em>
<span class="black-num">24.</span>knuckle of the space between the red oak and the red stars and all the linen between

<span class="black-num">25.</span>absence flaps on the floor like an eel out liberated from a buckle
<span class="black-num">26.</span>meaning buckles in grief, try again
<span class="black-num">27.</span>language like laundry whelps in the wind
<span class="black-num">28.</span>here goes a nothing into another nothing, the architecture of something
<span class="black-num">29.</span>words wing around the corner clear of the single lines you liberated from a poem
<span class="black-num">30.</span>arm-in-arm the world longs for a departed bride and dismantled groom 
<span class="black-num">31.</span>a poem roped upside down binds at the ankle 
<span class="black-num">32.</span>our hair entangled in a tree with a photograph of a the heart
<span class="black-num">33.</span>the green and bruised alchemy of the epistemological frown
<span class="black-num">34.</span>our lives pear pre-sheared and scored fruit on the table in Verona
<span class="black-num">35.</span>inspiration e-longs the stitching and syntax disappears on the gurney

<span class="black-num">36.</span>once we believed in the rocky shoal
<span class="black-num">37.</span>once we believed in the sky’s gravel state-by-state 
<span class="black-num">38.</span>once we believed temperance would build an intemperate nation 

iii

<em>the acorns, the hail, the feathers of the moon, the branches of the sun, the light on paper, the breath, the dog’s tail, the world bicycle tire, the ring-stained class of milk in late evening, the morning hair ,the post-oneira fatigue</em>
<em>the old questions scavenged, the no-more hyphenated dreams worded on the walls we once drew </em>

<em>all the falling things that sway between the hammocks of red grass swing in the same sloppy oration </em>
<em>as we run with the shadow-feather failing from the moon each another orphan flickering through the loam and liquor as the squirrels bark in the leaves</em>

the old questions rung with obduration 
the shadows of each of us flicker through the dying lawn

<em>shall we go on </em>
