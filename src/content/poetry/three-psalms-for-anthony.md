By recasting sacred language as an instrument of political indictment, Cardenal exposes the ideological function of propaganda, the complicity of political elites in the preparation for war, and the obscene disjunction between official invocations of peace and the material production of violence, including the existential threat of nuclear annihilation. His poetics of resistance reclaims spiritual solidarity as a force against the dehumanisation of the oppressed, transforming the ancient Psalm into a revolutionary counter-discourse that refuses the sanctification of state power and insists upon the ethical indivisibility of faith, class consciousness, and liberation."

<p><strong>Psalm 1</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--cardenal">Blessed the man who does not follow the Party's slogans
nor attend their rallies
nor sit at the gangster's table
nor with the War Council's Generals
Blessed the man who refuses to spy on his brother
or betray his classmate
Blessed the man who does not read their newspaper ads
nor listen to their radio shows
nor believe their slogans

<span class="cardenal-10">He will be like a tree planted by a spring</span></pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>Psalm 4</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--cardenal">Hear me because I invoke you God of my innocence
You will free me from the concentration camp

How long will our leaders be foolish?
How long until they quit speaking in slogans
and talking in pure propaganda?

They are many those who ask:
<span class="cardenal-20">who will free us from their atomic weapons?</span>
Lord let your serene countenance shine
over the Bombs

You have filled my heart with a joy
greater than that of the wine that they drink at their parties

As soon as I lie down I fall asleep
and I suffer neither nightmares nor insomnia
and I don't see the specters of my victims
I don't need Ambien
<span class="cardenal-20">because you Lord give me security</span></pre>

<p class="poem-next"><strong>Psalm 5</strong></p>

<pre class="poem-lines poem-lines--cardenal">Listen to my words oh Lord
<span class="cardenal-45">Hear my moans</span>
Listen to my protest
Because you are not a God that befriends dictators
nor a party to their politics
nor influenced by propaganda
nor are you in fellowship with gangsters

There is no sincerity in their speeches
nor in their press releases

They speak of peace in their speeches
while upping their production of war

They speak of peace at Peace Conferences
and in secret they prepare for war

<span class="cardenal-17">Their lying radios drone on all night</span>

Their desks are covered in criminal plans
<span class="cardenal-40">and sinister files</span>
But you will save me from their plans

They speak with the mouths of machine guns
Their resplendent tongues
<span class="cardenal-41">are bayonets...</span>

Punish them oh God
<span class="cardenal-33">ruin their politics</span>
confuse their memorandums
<span class="cardenal-40">impede their programs</span>

When the Alarm Siren sounds
you will be with me
you will be my refuge on the day of the Bomb

To he who do not believe in their commercial's lies
nor in their publicity campaigns nor in their political campaigns
<span class="cardenal-21">you bless him</span>
You surround him with your love
<span class="cardenal-52">like armor-plated tanks</span></pre>
