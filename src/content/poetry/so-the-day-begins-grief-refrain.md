**192**

So the day begins  
then keeps on going goes on  
and on above this  
spot that’s all


**124**

So the day begins  
laying a dedicated  
line for the people  
e.g., rope, gun, sword, rocket-


**191**

So the day begins,  
truly: soundless, handless, I  
slide through the flight of  
metaphors, seek the handhold


**195**

So the day begins  
I scream at it


**121**

So the day begins  
with good news. Keeping up mor-  
ale pays well and can  
extend the lifespan; jackpot.


**201**

So the day begins  
delirious I-I for-  
get|s lose|s itself  
in silver birchbark shred-


**140**

So the day begins  
in all the octopus’  
brains (nine), I have one,  
with one thought inside it


**105**

So the day begins  
and hands try not to tremble  
wind up the toy breath  
takes a step a step a step


**96**

So the day begins;  
there is no word that holds it  
back; no lever; the  
hand has no purpose here
