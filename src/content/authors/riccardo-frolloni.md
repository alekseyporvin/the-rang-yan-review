---
name: "Frolloni, Riccardo"
shortBio: "Italian poet, translator, and teacher (b. 1993, Macerata). His books include *Languide istantanee Polaroid* (Affinità Elettive, 2014), *Corpo striato* (Industria & Letteratura, 2021), and *Amigdala* (Nino Aragno Editore, 2024); with Giulio Zanet he published the artist’s book *Claustro* (2021). He has translated Richard Harrison and Ron Padgett, directs the series *Obtorto collo*, and founded Lo Spazio Letterario. He teaches Italian and Latin."
