She is the author of the poetry collection *Rebellion Box* (Radiant Press, 2023), the short fiction collection *Widow Fantasies* (Gordon Hill Press, 2024), long-listed for the Toronto Book Award, and the novel *The Unravelling of Ou* (Palimpsest Press, 2026), which was longlisted for the Giller Prize. She is a host on The New Books Network."
