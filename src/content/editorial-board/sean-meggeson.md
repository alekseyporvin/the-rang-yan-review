shortBio: "Sean G. Meggeson is a poet and audio/spoken word artist based in Toronto. His poetry has been published in a range of journals and magazines. Sean received the League of Canadian Poets Spoken Word Award in 2024 and was a finalist for the 2026 Montreal International Poetry Prize. His spoken word & soundscape work has been featured on the Ears Have Eyes podcast, NAISA radio, Radiophrenia 2026, and others. Sean's creative work explores the intersections of self, the socius, and language as textual sonics. He embraces the phenomenology of the liminal and tries to cultivate what UK poet Alan Fischer has called a \"confidence in lack.\""
fullBio: "Sean's critical stance is further informed by his career as a psychoanalytic psychotherapist whose training and clinical experience ranges from Freud and Lacan to the Interpersonal and to the psycho-social à la Deleuze & Guattari, Laing, and others. Sean has published and presented papers on inter-species intersubjectivity, neurodivergence, and psycho-synthesis. He has recently published a paper on the use of metaphor in psychoanalysis as seen through a Ricoeurian lens where he explores Ricoeur's proposition, \"The metaphor is not the enigma but the solution to the enigma.\""
plate: "As Rang Yan's Literature Editor for English Canada, Sean seeks to be attuned to the cultural expressions and conflicts that make up the diverse nation of Canada, a country that maybe came to know a little of itself in the 1960s through a literature of difference, a discourse of \"borderblur\" as first coined by Houédard in 1963 and later conceptualized by Canadian poets bpNichol and bill bissett."
