---
name: "Degli Esposti, Rita"
shortBio: "Italian poet, artist, and translator (b. Bologna; lives in Venice). She has taught Italian and Latin. Her books include *In livrea di transizione* (Supernova, 1990), *Dal margine del ciclone* (Porto dei Santi, 2000), *The Tiger in the Mirror* (Coyote Books, 2006), and *Letters to the Nightingale* (Longhouse, 2006). She has translated Cid Corman, Tom Raworth, Allen Ginsberg, Anne Waldman, Joanne Kyger, and Gary Snyder."
